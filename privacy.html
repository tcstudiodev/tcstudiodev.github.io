Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Video Factory is a private application used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to upload videos, update video settings, and read performance statistics for the owner's channel. It also reads public YouTube search results for research.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's private server. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app has no users other than its owner.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app's use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel owner via the email listed on the Google consent screen.
